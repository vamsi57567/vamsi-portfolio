Selected Projects

Case studies with measurable outcomes.

01 — ML & Risk Modeling
Predictive Model for Children at Risk of Elevated Blood Lead Levels
Problem
Risk needed modeling across 125,490 records with only 93 high-risk cases — a severe class imbalance problem that makes standard models unreliable.
Approach
Applied logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost alongside SMOTE and ADASYN resampling. After an early result appeared unusually strong, identified a data leakage issue, corrected it, and retuned models with RandomizedSearchCV before validating final results.
Outcome
Delivered validated models after catching and correcting a leakage issue that would have overstated performance — questioning results before trusting them.

02 — Trustworthy AI
RAG Based Hallucination Detection System
Problem
LLMs produce confident but factually incorrect outputs with no built-in verification layer.
Approach
Built an 8-layer retrieval-augmented generation system combining FAISS and BM25 hybrid retrieval with cross-encoder reranking. Added NLI-based consistency checks and uncertainty quantification.
Outcome
Improved factual accuracy from 59.05% to 62.75% on a 15,000-claim dataset. Reached 73.7% hallucination detection accuracy on adversarial test cases.
